QUESTIONS

Before you start.

When can I buy?

Foundation opens on January 5, 2027. Waitlist members get the $39 founding price for 72 hours. After that it's $49.

How is the program delivered?

Through a training app on your phone. After checkout you get an email with your access. Every workout, exercise demo, rest timer and weight log is in the app.

Do I need a gym?

Yes. Foundation is built for a standard gym with barbells, dumbbells, cables and common machines. Every exercise has substitutes for when equipment is busy or missing.

I'm a beginner. Is this for me?

Yes. A short quiz at signup puts you in the beginner or intermediate version. The beginner version uses fewer sets and simpler exercise variations in the first weeks.

Will I work with Kary one-on-one?

No. Foundation is fully self-guided: there's no messaging, check-ins or calls. The app tells you exactly what to do each session and how to progress.

How long do I have access?

6 months from purchase. That covers the 12 weeks plus time to repeat any week you miss.

Is it available in Spanish?

Yes. Program and exercise instructions are written in English and Spanish.

What if it's not for me?

You're covered by a 14-day money-back guarantee. Request a refund within 14 days of purchase and you'll get your money back.

Is it safe for me?

Foundation is a general program for healthy adults 18 and older. It isn't designed for pregnancy. If you have a medical condition, an injury or recent surgery, check with your doctor before you start.
